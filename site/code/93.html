Latvia
The fee set by an association or foundation for participation in sports competitions and for sports lessons. Provided that the purpose of the services provided is not to generate additional income for this association or foundation. (Section 52, Paragraph 1, Clauses 14.1 and 14.2 of the Law; https://likumi.lv/ta/en/en/id/253451)
