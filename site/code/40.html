What the member states say

Austria
From January 1, 2026, the supply of chemical, hormonal and mechanical contraceptives and of all kinds of feminine hygiene products are zero tax rated. Please notice that until December 31, 2025, all kinds of feminine hygiene products were subject to a reduced tax rate of 10% and the standard tax rate of 20% was applied to contraceptives.
