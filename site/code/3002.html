What the member states say

Estonia
This reduced rate applies only to toxins and other vaccines, toxins, and products similar to those in the presence of microorganisms (humans, animals and plant viruses and antiviruses, bacteriophages).
Greece
For tariff headings 2844, 3006, 3002, 3822 and 3824 reduced rate applies only on radioactive, shadow and other preparations or reagents for human medicine (for diagnostic or therapeutic purposes). After the cease of EU Directive 2020/2020 on 31/12/2022, the diagnostic rapid and PCR tests are also included in tariff heading 3822 .
Greece
Reduced rate applies only on medicaments for human medicine of tariff heading 3003 and 3004 and vaccines and immunological products for human medicine of tariff heading 3002
