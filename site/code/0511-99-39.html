What the member states say

Austria
Annex 1 to Sec. 10 of the Austrian VAT Act 1994 contains a list of goods falling under the reduced rate of 10% such as meal, fish, cereals, edible vegetable oils and so on (https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&amp;Gesetzesnummer=10004873&amp;Artikel=&amp;Paragraf=&amp;Anlage=1&amp;Uebergangsrecht=).
Austria
From January 1, 2026, the supply of chemical, hormonal and mechanical contraceptives and of all kinds of feminine hygiene products are zero tax rated. Please notice that until December 31, 2025, all kinds of feminine hygiene products were subject to a reduced tax rate of 10% and the standard tax rate of 20% was applied to contraceptives.
Germany
VAT - Import - Article 98 (2) and Annex III of the VAT Directive: Annex 2 of the German VAT Act contains a list of goods falling under the reduced rate of 7% such as products for monthly hygiene purposes and so on. For legal certainty please have a look at the German VAT Act.
