VAT rate for commodity code 2844 in the EU — Radioactive chemical elements and radioactive isotopes (including the fissile or fertile chemical elements and isotopes) and their compounds; mixtures and residues containing these products

Commodity code 2844 (Radioactive chemical elements and radioactive isotopes (including the fissile or fertile chemical elements and isotopes) and their compounds; mixtures and residues containing these products) carries rates from 6% to 9% across the 2 EU member states that report a rate for it. What the member states say

Estonia
This reduced rate applies only to radioactive chemical elements and isotopes and their compounds used for the treatment and diagnosis of diseases on importation by a person authorised to provide health care or on sale to that person.
Greece
For tariff headings 2844, 3006, 3002, 3822 and 3824 reduced rate applies only on radioactive, shadow and other preparations or reagents for human medicine (for diagnostic or therapeutic purposes). After the cease of EU Directive 2020/2020 on 31/12/2022, the diagnostic rapid and PCR tests are also included in tariff heading 3822 .
