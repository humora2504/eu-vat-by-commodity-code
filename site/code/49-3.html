What the member states say

Austria
The Austrian section of the journey in cross-border rail passenger transport is zero tax rated.
Belgium
Other transport of passengers as well as their accompanying unchecked luggage and animals - Royal Decree N° 20, Table A, category XXV
Finland
Domestic transport. In class 96.03.12 only carriage of corpses included.
France
Article 279 b quater of the general tax code
Italy
Passenger and their luggage transport services, excluding those set out in Table A, Part II-bis, No. 1-ter), and those exempt pursuant to Article 10, No. 14), of Presidential Decree No 633 of 1972 [excluding passenger transport services provided by lake means and taxi]. Suburban passenger transport service with the exception of transport for tourism and cruises.
Italy
Transport services provided by the means pursuant to Law No 1110 of 23 June 1927 and Royal Decree-Law No 1696 of 7 September 1939, converted into Law No. 8 of 5 January 1939 [transport by drag lifts, sledges and other means of transport without rails]. Urban passenger transport service with the exception of transport for tourism and exempt transport.
Netherlands

Portugal
