What the member states say

Greece
For tariff headings 2844, 3006, 3002, 3822 and 3824 reduced rate applies only on radioactive, shadow and other preparations or reagents for human medicine (for diagnostic or therapeutic purposes). After the cease of EU Directive 2020/2020 on 31/12/2022, the diagnostic rapid and PCR tests are also included in tariff heading 3822 .
Greece
For tariff headings of goods intended for the service of persons with disabilities needs, the reduced rate applies only on sugar measuring tapes (3822),luminous system alarm (8531), light signal receiver (8517)
Croatia
Applies to medical equipment, aids and other appliances normally intended to alleviate or treat disability, for the exclusive personal use of the disabled people, prescribed by an act of general application on orthopaedic and other aids.
Hungary
human medical products, but the standard rate is applicable to products for veterinary purposes
Latvia
Certain medical devices intended only for individual use by persons with functional body disorders. (Section 42, Paragraph 2 of the Law; https://likumi.lv/ta/en/en/id/253451)
Malta
- Medical accessories: This reduced rate does not apply to medical accessories falling under CN codes 3001 and 3002 where such items are used for therapeutic or prophylactic purposes; - For code 3307 90 00, the reduced rate applies solely to solutions for contact lenses. - In May 2020, the reduced rate has been extended to protective face masks and visors, excluding diving equipment.
Slovakia
Only selected items. Exhaustive list can be found in Annex 7 of Act No. 222/2004 Coll. on Value Added Tax as amended via the following link https://www.mfsr.sk/en/taxes-customs-accounting/indirect-taxes/vat/
