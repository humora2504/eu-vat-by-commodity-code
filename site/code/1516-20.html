VAT rate for commodity code 1516 20 in the EU — Vegetable fats and oils and their fractions

Commodity code 1516 20 (Vegetable fats and oils and their fractions) carries rates from 9% to 10% across the 3 EU member states that report a rate for it. The full table is below, and the same data is available as JSON.

What the member states say

Austria
Annex 1 to Sec. 10 of the Austrian VAT Act 1994 contains a list of goods falling under the reduced rate of 10% such as meal, fish, cereals, edible vegetable oils and so on (https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/NormDokument.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&amp;Gesetzesnummer=10004873&amp;Artikel=&amp;Paragraf=&amp;Anlage=1&amp;Uebergangsrecht=).
Italy
Partially or totally hydrogenated animal or vegetable oils and fats and animal or vegetable oils and fats solidified or hardened by any other process, including refined, but not prepared, intended for human or animal consumption. 1516 10, 1516 20 if for human or animal consumption
Netherlands
Tabel I prov. a.1: foodstuff for human consumption, products used for preparation of human foodstuff that are wholly/partially incorporated in these foodstuff, supplements/substitutes for human foodstuff; Excluded: living animals, except if consumed raw.
