What the member states say

Royal Decree N° 20, Table A, category XVII The reduced rate only applies to: - sterile hypodermic syringes for single use, intended for the injection of insulin solutions and graduated for this purpose in international units of insulin; disposable sterile needles for insulin injector pens: 9018 31 -intrauterine IUDs (pessaries): 9018 9084

Applies to medical equipment, aids and other appliances normally intended to alleviate or treat disability, for the exclusive personal use of the disabled people, prescribed by an act of general application on orthopaedic and other aids.
Netherlands
Tabel I prov. a.36 and b.1: aids that are usually used for the subcutaneous administration of insulin, with the exception of syringes and needles that are apparently also suitable for other purposes; aids that are usually used for the self-diagnosis of blood sugar levels. Only included are the mentioned devices specifically developed for diabetics and offered as such. Generally applicable aids
