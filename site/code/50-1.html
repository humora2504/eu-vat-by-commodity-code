What the member states say

Belgium
Sea passenger transport, international air passenger transport, transport of luggage and cars accompanied by passengers during sea passenger transport and international air passenger transport - Article 41, § 1, 1° of the Belgian VAT Code
Denmark
Taxis, scheduled busses, trains and air transport are exempted. In connection with international transportation of passengers the exemption also covers accompanying luggage. Passenger transport in tourist busses and similar vehicles (CPA 49.39.32, 49.39.33, 49.39.34) are subject to VAT in Denmark (Standard rate).
Finland
Domestic transport. In class 96.03.12 only carriage of corpses included.
Italy
Passenger transport services provided by means of transport entitled to execute sea, lake, river and lagoon transport services. Urban passenger transport service by water with the exception of transport for tourism, cruises and exempt transport.
Italy
Passenger and their luggage transport services, excluding those set out in Table A, Part II-bis, No. 1-ter), and those exempt pursuant to Article 10, No. 14), of Presidential Decree No 633 of 1972 [excluding passenger transport services provided by lake means and taxi]. Suburban passenger transport service with the exception of transport for tourism and cruises.
Netherlands
Tabel I prov. b.9: private bus transport; public transport and taxi transport as referred to in Article 1 of the 'Wet personenvervoer 2000 (Dutch 'Passenger Transport Act 2000'), taxi transport of persons by road other than by car, and the transport of persons by aircraft if the place of departure and the place of destination are located in the Netherlands insofar as this transport takes place by
Portugal
International transport of passengers is subject to a zero rate.
Sweden
Domestic transports of passengers are subject to the reduced rate, please see chapter 9 section 8 in the Swedish VAT Act (https://www.riksdagen.se/sv/dokument-lagar/dokument/svensk-forfattningssamling/mervardesskattelag-2023200_sfs-2023-200#K9). International transports of passengers are exempted.
