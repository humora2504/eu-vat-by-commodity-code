Ireland
9% applies to brochures, leaflets, programmes, catalogues, directories, maps, hydrographic and similar charts, and printed music- other than in book or booklet form; excluding matter devoted to advertising. The rate for these items was temporarily reduced from 13.5% to 9% from 1 November 2020 to 31 August 2023.
