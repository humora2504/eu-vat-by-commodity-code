VAT rate for commodity code 3002 42 00 in the EU — Vaccines for veterinary medicine

Commodity code 3002 42 00 (Vaccines for veterinary medicine) carries rates from 9% to 10% across the 3 EU member states that report a rate for it. The full table is below, and the same data is available as JSON.

What the member states say

Italy
Medicines ready for human or veterinary use, including homeopathic products; pharmaceutical substances and medications which pharmacies must be equipped with according to the official pharmacopoeia.
Netherlands
Tabel I prov. a.7: veterinary medicines as referred to in the 'Wet dieren' (Dutch 'Animal Act'), which are used in or on the animal itself (in vivo use). Excluded: products that are necessary to make a diagnosis and that are applied to tissue or body fluids originating from animals (in vitro use) are excluded; products that are used in the animal's living environment.
