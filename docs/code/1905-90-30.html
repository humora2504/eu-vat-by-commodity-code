What the member states say

Austria
From July 1, 2026, the tax rate of 4.9% applies to the foodstuffs listed in Annex 3 to Sec. 10 of the Austrian VAT Act 1994. Please note, among other things, that the reduced tax rate of 10% continues to apply to CN subheadings 0703 1011, 0709 5400, 0709 5500, and 0709 5600.
Estonia
This reduced rate applies only to food for special medical purposes within the meaning of the Food Act.
Croatia
Reduced rate applies to all types of bread, and all types of milk (cow, sheep, goat) placed on the market under the same name in the liquid state, fresh, pasteurized, homogenized, condensed (other than sour milk, yoghurt, kefir, chocolate milk and other milk products), substitute for mother’s milk.
Italy
Pasta products; crackers and rusks; bread, seafood biscuits and other ordinary bakery products, including those containing ingredients and substances permitted by Title III of Law No 580 of 4 July 1967, without added sugar, honey, eggs or cheese
Slovakia
All foodstuffs except the selected items taxed at 5 % rate and selected foods with increased sugar and salt content. These are goods primarily intended for human consumption or for the production of goods suitable for human consumption.
Slovakia
Only selected items - fresh or chilled meat, live fish, fresh or chilled fish, milk, butter, bread and other selected foodstuffs (inluding gluten-free pastries, flour and bread).
