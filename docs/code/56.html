Greece
The reduced rate (RR) applies to restaurant and catering services, excluding night clubs. Moreover, the RR applies to the delivery or take-away of non-alcoholic beverages (nAB) from companies that are dealing with restaurant and catering services. The standard rate still applies to alcoholic beverages IN ANY CASE (i.e. serving, delivery or take away) and ONLY in the serving of nAB
Finland
