What the member states say

Netherlands
Tabel I prov. a.6: medicines as referred to in article 1-1-B of the 'Geneesmiddelenwet' (Dutch 'Medicine Act') for which a marketing authorization as referred to in article 1, section III of that act has been granted or for which a marketing authorization is not required pursuant to article 40-3-A to G of that act; contraceptives; infusion fluids; kidney dialyses concentrates; and inhalation gases
Portugal
A few pharmaceutical products taxed at 23% (veterinarian purposes and vitamins, per ex.)
Romania
The reduced TVA rate is applied for the supply of medicines for human use. See art. 291 para. (2) letter a) of the Fiscal Code.
