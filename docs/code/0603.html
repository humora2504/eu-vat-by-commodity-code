VAT rate for commodity code 0603 in the EU — Cut flowers and flower buds of a kind suitable for bouquets or for ornamental purposes, fresh, dried, dyed, bleached, impregnated or otherwise prepared

Commodity code 0603 (Cut flowers and flower buds of a kind suitable for bouquets or for ornamental purposes, fresh, dried, dyed, bleached, impregnated or otherwise prepared) carries rates from 8% to 13% across the 7 EU member states that report a rate for it.
