What the member states say

Croatia
Applies to medical equipment, aids and other appliances normally intended to alleviate or treat disability, for the exclusive personal use of the disabled people, prescribed by an act of general application on orthopaedic and other aids.
Luxembourg
Medical equipment, aids and other appliances normally intended to alleviate or treat disability, for the exclusive personal use of the disabled, including the repair of such goods
Romania
Supply of prostheses and their accessories, with the exception of dental prostheses exempt from tax under art.292 para.(1) lett.(b) and supply of orthopedic products. When those goods are imported from a third territory or a third country, the reduced VAT rate is conditioned by submission of the Customs Notice for medical devices or the Declaration of conformity of the medical device manufacturer
