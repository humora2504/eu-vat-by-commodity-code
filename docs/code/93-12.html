What the member states say

Belgium
Services provided by institutions not systemically aiming to make a profit and assigning revenues are only used to cover operational expenses according to article 44, § 2, 3° of the Belgian VAT Code. The VAT exemption only applies to the codes 93.11 and 93.12 if the latter conditions are met
Belgium
Admission to and use of sports facilities according to Royal Decree N° 20, Table A, category XXVIII with the exception of the: - right to use automatic entertainment devices - putting at disposal of movable goods
Czechia
Admission to sporting events; use of indoor and outdoor sports facilities for sports activities; services related to the operation of recreational parks and beaches; operation of ski slopes.
Netherlands
Tabel I prov. b.3 and b.14, f: the granting of (physical) access to spectators of sports competitions, demonstrations and the like. Sports competitions are events where people actively participate in sports.
